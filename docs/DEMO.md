# 90-second demonstration

Use a clear wooden elephant photo and a stable connection. Avoid repeated AI calls: one identification is enough. If presenting locally, `?mock=1` provides a visibly labelled development fixture and simulated submission.

| Time    | Action and point                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0–10 s  | Open FairFare. Name the Jaipur pilot and visible demonstration disclosure.                                                                                                           |
| 10–25 s | Scan or choose the wooden elephant photograph. Explain that ordinary scans remain temporary.                                                                                         |
| 25–40 s | Identify once. Confirm/edit wood, approximate 10 cm height, plain finish and local craft shop.                                                                                       |
| 40–60 s | Compare. Show the database-calculated illustrative range, source count and one expanded synthetic listing. Change the seller quote to demonstrate context without accusing a seller. |
| 60–72 s | Open purchase reporting. Show reuse of attributes, optional photo consent and pending review. Do not describe the illustrative range as a verified market price.                     |
| 72–82 s | Briefly show taxi payment guidance, the ride agreement note and monument official links/unavailable current fees.                                                                    |
| 82–90 s | Open the separate contributor desk. End with a local observation entering trusted review, never instant publication.                                                                 |

For a live submission, use an honest real observation or clearly label a test, verify it in Supabase and reject it afterward. Do not submit fictional observations as real purchases.
