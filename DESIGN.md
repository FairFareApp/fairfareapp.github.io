# FairFare design direction

Source: the owner's build brief and supplied logo. Brand colours and warm surfaces are an explicit owner direction, including the terracotta accent exception to taste-skill's default palette rule.

Reading: a quick-use travel app for Jaipur visitors, warm consumer language. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

Dealt direction: Pastel consumer, seed 521539996. Keep brand palette. Use Manrope for open, readable forms and distinctive tabular money figures. No framework or build pipeline.

Screen questions: home, what can I check in Jaipur? Scan, what is this object? Compare, which observations are actually comparable? Transport, what should I confirm before paying? Monuments, where can I verify the right ticket? Contribution, what evidence makes this observation useful?

Inventory: Jaipur pilot; carved wooden elephants; size in centimetres; wood versus claimed sandalwood; plain versus painted finish; street, shop and boutique contexts; seller asking versus paid prices; synthetic versus researched provenance; pending moderation; transient scan images; a price-tag logo; rupee ranges.

Idea: FairFare's price tag becomes an evidence label: the range is always attached to its comparable observations, source type and limitations.

Face: supplied green and terracotta tag, warm white ground, sage-tinted context labels, Manrope figures. Signature: an evidence-first price panel with editable product attributes and individually expandable comparison records.

Decisions:

- Warm white keeps outdoor reading comfortable; dark neutral text supplies contrast.
- Sage identifies local context; terracotta marks the one primary action. Accessible darker variants supply small text and button fills.
- A compact top navigation keeps scan and travel guidance within reach; contributor routes use a separate work-focused navigation.
- A split working area pairs the photo or task with the evidence it needs, stacking on phones and using an intermediate tablet layout.
- Cards group one job or one product, never invented performance statistics.
- FontAwesome solid icons from CDN name actual actions and travel modes; every icon-only control has an accessible label.
- Spacing uses 4/8px increments, with larger gaps between jobs than between related fields.
- Radii: 20px task panels, 10px controls, small status labels. Shadows are reserved for dialogs.
- Motion: short pressed/hover feedback and loading progress only; reduced motion removes transitions.
- Light/dark themes use neutral surfaces for day and evening use, with separately checked contrast.
- No generated people, testimonials, product photos or trust metrics. Synthetic prices are labelled on every relevant screen.

Asset conversion: crop the supplied logo and preserve its own background with rounded transparent corners for favicon and install icons, as requested by the owner.
