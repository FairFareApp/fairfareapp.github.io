# FairFare design direction

Source: the owner's build brief and supplied logo. Brand colours and warm surfaces are an explicit owner direction, including the terracotta accent exception to taste-skill's default palette rule.

Redesign reading, 19 November 2026: a distracted visitor needs one immediate action. ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. Calm, task-first consumer interface; the owner's brief explicitly asks to remove the marketing composition. Preserve the established Pastel consumer palette and Manrope, reduce visible copy, and use progressive disclosure.

Dealt direction: Pastel consumer, seed 521539996. Keep brand palette. Use Manrope for open, readable forms and distinctive tabular money figures. No framework or build pipeline.

Screen questions: home, what can I check in Jaipur? Scan, what is this object? Compare, which observations are actually comparable? Transport, what should I confirm before paying? Monuments, where can I verify the right ticket? Contribution, what evidence makes this observation useful?

Inventory: Jaipur pilot; carved wooden elephants; size in centimetres; wood versus claimed sandalwood; plain versus painted finish; street, shop and boutique contexts; seller asking versus paid prices; synthetic versus researched provenance; pending moderation; transient scan images; a price-tag logo; rupee ranges.

Idea: FairFare's price tag becomes an evidence label: the range is always attached to its comparable observations, source type and limitations.

Face: supplied green and terracotta tag, warm white ground, sage-tinted context labels, Manrope figures. Signature: an evidence-first price panel with editable product attributes and individually expandable comparison records.

Decisions:

- Warm white keeps outdoor reading comfortable; dark neutral text supplies contrast.
- Sage identifies local context; terracotta marks the one primary action. Accessible darker variants supply small text and button fills.
- A compact top navigation keeps scan and travel guidance within reach; contributor routes use a separate work-focused navigation.
- A split working area pairs the photo or task with the evidence it needs, stacking on phones and using an intermediate tablet layout.
- Cards group one job or one product, never invented performance statistics.
- FontAwesome solid icons from CDN name actual actions and travel modes; every icon-only control has an accessible label.
- Spacing uses 4/8px increments, with larger gaps between jobs than between related fields.
- Radii: 20px task panels, 10px controls, small status labels. Shadows are reserved for dialogs.
- Motion: short pressed/hover feedback and loading progress only; reduced motion removes transitions.
- Light/dark themes use neutral surfaces for day and evening use, with separately checked contrast.
- No generated people, testimonials or trust metrics. Four explicitly commissioned/generated wooden-elephant finish illustrations support synthetic comparison listings; the user specifically permits illustrative images in this redesign. They are never described as purchase photos or independent evidence.

Redesign decisions: the scan action owns the home screen, using the logo's clipped price-tag corner rather than a decorative logo card. Sage provides its calm large action surface; terracotta identifies the next action inside a workflow. Price figures own the result screen. Navigation rows have equal geometry because they are parallel destinations. Contributor steps show one decision group at a time and retain drafts in memory. The centered home canvas is deliberately capped at 820px with equal margins, rather than stretched into a dashboard; the inspector's sidebar inference is inapplicable to this top-navigation app. No gradients, glow, background patterns or decorative motion. Tiny status/navigation text is at least 11px; input text is 16px to avoid iOS zoom. Actual sourced monument photographs are hosted locally, with attribution/license in visit details.

Asset conversion: crop the supplied logo and preserve its own background with rounded transparent corners for favicon and install icons, as requested by the owner.

## Colour and motion follow-up

Design read for 20 November 2026. A Jaipur pocket guide for visitors making quick decisions, with colours drawn from lacquered bangles, turmeric, indigo and foliage. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2. The Hawa Mahal photograph makes the city recognisable at launch. The photograph is by Wander-earth, with the original and licence linked in About FairFare.

- Use lac for scanning and the main price figure, leaf green for preparation, indigo for ride guidance, and turmeric for bargaining, ticket figures and the submission receipt. Keep the app canvas neutral so the colours mark useful information.
- Use the clipped corner of the supplied price-tag mark on the home banner, action tiles, tip cards and receipt stamp. Keep dense forms on quiet surfaces.
- Layer the tip cards and move the top one with the pointer. A brief drag settles back. A swipe or tap advances one card while preserving the link and a way to return. Honour reduced motion and keep each card usable with a keyboard.
- Show submission feedback while the request is pending. Animate the receipt check only after acceptance for review.

Reference study. Onto gives destinations distinct photographs and clear actions. Duolingo ties short animations to a completed exercise. Revolut uses swiping to browse physical card designs. Phantom puts the quote and primary action within reach and moves the technical details into a disclosure. FairFare applies those patterns to its own travel tasks. ## Home, rides and visit cards

A Jaipur pocket guide with a larger photographic opening and shared horizontal card motion. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

- Home opens with three generated still lifes made from the supplied tag silhouette and its green, ivory and clay layers. Campaign subjects represent the actual scan, ride and monument tasks. The product scope and illustrative pricing sit next to the real camera and gallery controls.
- Use one swipe surface for campaigns, tips and visits. Left moves forward, right moves back, and tapping moves forward. The home carousel loops while visible and has a pause control. Tips end with a replay option. Vertical scrolling stays available.
- Monument photographs carry the current visitor category and ticket link. Labelled card edges show what is behind the current place. A details disclosure keeps source dates and image attribution within reach.
- Give the ride pages different feature colours and the same mode control. A ride checklist shows checked items, and a short scenario gives feedback on a travel choice. Keep the local fare calculator and sources within reach.
- The primary navigation carries the five destinations and More. The information pages use regular routes and keep the active navigation visible.

## FairFare first

The home campaign leads with the FairFare tag and the value of comparing, preparing and contributing. Five generated paper still lifes share clay, ivory and leaf green. A continuous horizontal rail loops using matching end slides. It yields to touch, focus, hover and reduced motion. There is no visible carousel toolbar.

The compass for visual hierarchy is the action contrast. The square camera button is the largest tool on the home scan panel. The Jaipur card has a visible Open travel tips action and earns a check stamp on completion. All active tabs use the same sage treatment under the diagonal saffron, ivory and green header. The shade and type scale remain consistent across guides and actions.

The venue stack promotes the next photograph while the front photograph travels out and tucks behind the other cards. The active photo and ticket data share a constant overlay. The ride progress fill, Jaipur check and successful price reveal respond to completed actions. All retain their final state with reduced motion.

## Installed launch and visit stack

The installed app introduces the tag with a short swing and a moving saffron, cream and leaf wash. Browser tabs go straight to content. The launch dismisses automatically and skips motion when the device requests it.

The home photo action spans the panel, with Gallery underneath and a taller Jaipur invitation. The privacy footer belongs to Home. More still provides a route to the policy.

All six monument cards occupy visible layers. The closest two backing titles fade as their cards move between depths. The same physical path plays in reverse for a backward swipe. The visit order changes after the motion settles, while vertical scrolling remains available.

## Quicker visit cards and launch handoff

All six photo edges remain visible on the monument stack. The active card carries the title and fare. A shorter travel path and a small settle motion give taps and swipes a quicker response.

Android draws a static launch frame from installed metadata. The manifest, page theme and first frame of the FairFare animation use the same warm cream.
